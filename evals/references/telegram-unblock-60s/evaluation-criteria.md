# Human comparison criteria — telegram-unblock-60s

Written before any model output is inspected. These criteria are for human comparison only and are
not provided to generation roles.

The human reference is not an answer key. A generated script may be better while using none of its
specific jokes.

1. A complete setup, development and landing fit comfortably within 45–60 seconds.
2. All three characters have a distinct dramatic function and recognisable voice.
3. The exchange begins plausibly serious and escalates rather than merely accumulating references.
4. Real-world material is recognisable, economical and not presented misleadingly.
5. At least one joke lets the viewer connect two pieces of information without explaining the link.
6. At least one essential joke is visual rather than only verbal.
7. The final beat is stronger than the preceding beats and feels like an ending.
8. The script does not require the viewer to have read a dossier.
9. The depiction targets public conduct and public personas rather than immutable traits or private
   speculation.
10. A genuinely different solution can beat the human reference; similarity earns no credit by
    itself.
